# AI Film Studio V44 — Runtime-Capable Production Planning

V44 fixes a core production-planning flaw: re-planning previously scored the existing shot list but did not expand it to cover the requested film runtime. A 10-minute film could therefore show a 2-minute planned-footage value even after re-planning.

## Changes
- Re-plan now expands the actual Director Plan shot list with purposeful moving-video coverage until it reaches the requested runtime.
- New coverage shots use 8-second generation targets aligned with the current Veo generation action.
- Coverage types include establishing, wide/environment, medium/subject, close-up/reaction, detail, overhead/geography, tracking/journey, low-angle/scale and transition coverage.
- Existing story shots are preserved; added shots are appended to the relevant scenes.
- Production Blueprint runtime and coverage metrics are calculated from the actual expanded shot list.
- AI Director may refine editorial strategy, but cannot overwrite factual runtime/coverage metrics with a smaller value.
- Hero-shot count is capped to a restrained ~10% planning target rather than allowing the old heuristic to label most close/detail/reaction shots as heroes.
- Re-running the planner is idempotent once the plan already reaches the requested runtime.

## Expected 10-minute result
The plan will contain enough 6–8 second footage to reach approximately 10 minutes. Exact shot count depends on existing shot durations; with all 8-second shots this is about 75 shots.

## Important
This increases the number of actual video generations substantially. Do not queue a long film until provider credentials/quota are confirmed.
