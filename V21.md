# AI Film Studio V21 — Professional AI Editor & Transition Engine

V21 upgrades the timeline from a basic edit-decision layer into a professional, non-destructive editorial control surface.

## Added
- Scene-aware AI Cut operation.
- Manual transition selection: cut, dissolve, fade, fade-to-black, fade-to-white, wipe left, slide right and smooth slide.
- Per-transition duration control.
- Non-destructive split operation at the selected clip midpoint.
- Clip reordering and timing controls retained.
- EDL export retained.
- Timeline schema version 2 with transitionDuration.
- Render worker honors supported xfade transitions when rendering chunks.
- Scene boundaries receive restrained dissolves/fades while close/hero shots remain hard cuts.

## Design rule
Transitions are editorial punctuation, not decoration. The AI editor should prefer clean cuts unless story rhythm, scene change, time passage or emotional tone benefits from a transition.

## Compatibility
V18 reference matching, V19 production planning and V20 temporal continuity remain intact. Existing PumpOS is untouched.

## Limitation
The current render worker implements supported FFmpeg xfade transitions. A future professional compositor can add more advanced transition graphs, optical-flow effects, speed ramps and keyframed transforms without changing the timeline contract.
