# V13 — Self-Sufficient Production Media Pipeline

V13 closes the media durability gap between generation and rendering.

## Automatic persistence
When `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` and `projectId` are available:
- Veo video results are copied to Blob and the shot points to the durable URL.
- Natural narration is copied to Blob and saved on the project.
- Music and SFX are copied to Blob and saved on their tracks.
- Neon records every durable media asset.

## Render safety
With `REQUIRE_DURABLE_MEDIA=1`, render creation requires durable media and performs a preflight persistence pass before building the manifest.

## Recovery
- Render callbacks update the durable render job and project state.
- Render chunks are stored in Neon.
- `/api/render/jobs/:id/chunks` exposes chunk status.
- Render Studio polls chunk state and exposes retry after failure.

## Development fallback
Without Blob/Neon, local JSON and local rendering remain available when explicitly enabled. Production should use Neon + Blob + the external FFmpeg worker.
