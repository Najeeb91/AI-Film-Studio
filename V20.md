# AI Film Studio V20 — Advanced Continuity & Temporal Consistency

V20 adds sequence-level continuity rather than evaluating shots only in isolation.

## Added
- Temporal QC comparing current-shot frames with the previous-shot frames.
- Identity drift, environment drift, wardrobe drift and prop drift scoring.
- Transition quality and explicit continuity-break reporting.
- Scene sequence metadata in ContinuityState: sequence index, previous/next shot and expected progression.
- Locked continuity elements: characters, location, wardrobe, props, references and visual style.
- `/api/qc/temporal` endpoint.
- Project editor `Sequence QC` action on generated shots.
- Multimodal analysis through AI Gateway with heuristic fallback.

## Behavior
A shot can pass individual visual QC but still fail Sequence QC if the cut creates an unexplained identity, wardrobe, environment, prop, lighting or spatial continuity break.

The first shot of a film has no predecessor and therefore establishes the baseline instead of receiving a false temporal comparison.

V17/V18/V19 functionality remains intact and PumpOS is untouched.
